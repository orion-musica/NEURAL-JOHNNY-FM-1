# Your own loops (optional)

Put `.txt` files here and run `python3 johnny/tools/train_model.py` to teach the model your taste.
They are mixed into the built-in hypnotic-techno training set (each loop counted 20 times).

One loop per line, **32 or 64** steps (2 or 4 bars of 16ths), separated by spaces:
`.` is a rest, a number is semitones from the tonic (−12 … 24). `#` starts a comment.

    # A minor, 1-bar motif + answer
    0 . . 3 . . 7 . 0 . . -2 . . 7 . 0 . . 3 . . 7 . 5 . . 3 . . -5 .
